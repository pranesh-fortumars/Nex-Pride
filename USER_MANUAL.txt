# NexPride Platform - Complete User Documentation

Welcome to the NexPride User Documentation. This guide covers the complete end-to-end functionality of the platform across all three user roles: **Job Seekers**, **Employers (Factories)**, and **Administrators**.

---

## 🌟 Pros and Benefits of the Application

The NexPride platform is built to solve critical hiring challenges in the textile industry. Key advantages include:
1. **Targeted for the Textile Industry**: Custom-built with industry-specific categories, departments, and designations (e.g., Sewing, Merchandising, Cutting), eliminating the clutter of generic job boards.
2. **Mobile-First & Multilingual Support**: Ensures blue-collar workers and non-technical users can easily navigate the app in their regional language directly from their smartphones.
3. **High Security & Trust**: A robust Admin verification process ensures only legitimate, registered factories can post jobs, preventing scams and fake recruiters.
4. **Transparent Communication**: Direct calling and WhatsApp integration allow employers and candidates to connect instantly without friction.
5. **Streamlined Hiring Workflow**: Employers can easily track candidate statuses (Shortlist, Hire, Reject) and archive jobs once filled, keeping their dashboard clean and organized.
6. **Detailed Job Insights**: Seekers get granular details about perks (Tea cash, free food, hostel, ESI/EPF) upfront, leading to higher retention rates.

## 1. Getting Started: Authentication (Login & Signup)

The platform provides a secure and unified authentication system.

### 1.1 Sign Up
- **Flow**: Users choose their role upon sign-up: "I am looking for a job" (Seeker) or "I want to hire" (Employer).
- **Credentials**: Users can sign up using their Phone Number + OTP (if supported) or Email + Password.
- **Onboarding**: After signing up, users are immediately taken to their respective onboarding screens to complete their profile setup.

### 1.2 Login
- **Flow**: Existing users can log in using their registered Email/Password or Phone/OTP.
- **Role Redirection**: Based on the assigned role in the system, users are automatically routed to their specific dashboard:
  - Seekers → Home Page / Job Search
  - Employers → Employer Dashboard
  - Admins → Admin Dashboard

## 2. Job Seeker Module (For Workers & Staff)

This module is designed for individuals looking for jobs in the textile industry.

### 2.1 The Home Page
- **Search & Filter**: A comprehensive search bar allows seekers to search for jobs by title, location, and department.
- **Categories**: Quick links to browse jobs by major categories (e.g., Merchandising, Sewing, HR, Production).
- **Latest Opportunities**: Displays a list of currently active jobs. (Note: Jobs with expired interview dates or closed statuses are hidden to ensure only relevant opportunities are shown).

### 2.2 Digital Resume (Profile)
Seekers must build their profile before applying to jobs.
- **Personal Details**: Name, Phone, Email, Address, Date of Birth.
- **Professional Details**: Total experience, current role, and core skills.
- **Preferences**: Preferred work location, expected salary, and shift timing preferences.
- **Documents**: Upload profile picture and government ID proofs.

### 2.3 Finding and Applying for Jobs
- **Job Search Page (/jobs)**: Seekers can filter open positions by location, experience required, and job type.
- **Job Details Page**: Provides comprehensive information including:
  - Job Title, Company Name, Location.
  - Salary range and payout schedule (Daily/Weekly/Monthly).
  - Perks and Benefits (e.g., ESI/EPF, Free Food, Transport, Hostel).
  - Interview dates and timings.
- **Applying**: Seekers can apply directly via the "Apply Now" button.
  - *Note*: If the interview date has passed, the button becomes disabled and reads "🔒 Interview Passed".

### 2.4 Application Tracking
- Seekers can track their job applications. Statuses include:
  - **Applied**: Sent to employer.
  - **Shortlisted**: Employer has reviewed and is interested.
  - **Hired**: Seeker got the job.
  - **Rejected**: Application was unsuccessful.

## 3. Employer Module (For Factories & Companies)

This module allows factories to post jobs, manage applications, and hire workers.

### 3.1 Factory Profile & Verification
Employers must be verified by an Admin before their jobs go live.
- **Company Details**: Company Name, Industry, Size, Founded Year, and Description.
- **Verification Proof**:
  - **GST Registration**: Must provide a valid GST number.
  - **Entrance Proof**: Upload photos of the factory gate/outside.
  - **Inside Photos**: Upload photos of the workspace to build trust with candidates.
- **Status**: Will show as `PENDING` until an Admin approves the profile, at which point it becomes `APPROVED`.

### 3.2 Employer Dashboard
The central hub for employers to manage hiring.
- **Metrics Overview**: View total active jobs, total applications received, and hired candidates.
- **Post a Job**: Create a new job listing.
  - Set Job Title, Department, Openings.
  - Set Salary, Work Type, Shift Timings, Benefits.
  - **Crucial**: Set Interview Start and End dates to let candidates know when to walk in.
- **Job Management**:
  - View all posted jobs.
  - **Close/Archive Jobs**: If a position is filled, employers can close or archive the job (must provide a reason). Archived jobs will stop receiving new applications.
- **Candidate Management**:
  - View applications for each job.
  - Review Seeker profiles (skills, experience, location).
  - Update candidate status (Shortlist, Hire, Reject).
  - Call or message candidates directly from the dashboard.

## 4. Admin Module (Platform Administration)

The Super Admin dashboard provides complete control over the platform's ecosystem, ensuring safety, compliance, and quality.

### 4.1 Dashboard Overview & Metrics
- Real-time statistics on total factories, workers, jobs posted, and active reports.

### 4.2 Verify Companies (Factory Approval)
- Admins review all new Employer registrations.
- They can inspect GST details, location, contact info, and uploaded photos.
- **Actions**:
  - `Approve`: Allows the employer's jobs to go live.
  - `Reject`: Denies the employer access if credentials are fake.
  - `Mark as Trusted`: Gives the company a special "Trusted" badge for high reliability.

### 4.3 Job Approvals (Content Moderation)
- All new job postings require Admin approval before appearing on the public search.
- Admins ensure the salary, description, and benefits comply with platform standards.
- If an employer is not yet verified, the system warns the admin that the employer must be approved first.

### 4.4 Job Performance & Analytics
- View detailed analytics for specific jobs (views, reach, application conversion rates).
- See a list of which registered users viewed a specific job.

### 4.5 Safety Center (Reports)
- If a user (Seeker or Employer) reports a profile for being fake, abusive, or suspicious, it appears here.
- Admins can review the report description and uploaded screenshot evidence.
- Actions include dismissing the report or blocking/suspending the offending account.

### 4.6 Designations & Settings
- **Designations**: Manage the predefined dropdown list of job titles and departments that employers can choose from when posting jobs.
- **Settings**: Manage global platform configurations (e.g., Support contact details, hours of operation).

### 4.7 My Profile & Manage Admins
- **My Profile**: Admins can update their Full Name and Date of Birth.
- **Manage Admins**: Super Admins can securely provision new administrator accounts.
  - Requires inputting the new admin's Name, Email, Password, and DOB.
  - The system creates the account securely in the background without interrupting the active admin session.

## 5. Global Features

### 5.1 Multilingual Support
- The platform includes a language toggle (e.g., English / Tamil), ensuring that factory workers can navigate the app in their native language comfortably.

### 5.2 Responsive Design
- The entire platform is built with a mobile-first approach, meaning all dashboards, resumes, and search pages work seamlessly on smartphones, which are the primary devices used by job seekers.

### 5.3 Notifications & Support
- Users receive real-time UI toasts for success and error actions.
- A dedicated support page allows users to contact the platform's help desk via WhatsApp, Phone, or Email based on the Admin settings.
